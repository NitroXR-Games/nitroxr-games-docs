# Vibe Record: Maze-Core to Expansion

## Strategic Intent
The project evolved through a "Traction-first" approach, moving from a basic prototype to a social platform.

### Lap 1: Core Engine (Stealth/Precision)
- **Focus**: Absolute collision and basic loop.
- **Outcome**: Zero-clipping prototype.

### Lap 2: Experience Layer (Market-Capture)
- **Focus**: Visuals and Solvability.
- **Outcome**: Recursive backtracking mazes and XR HUD.

### Lap 3: Competitive Edge (Market-Capture)
- **Focus**: Replayability and Competition.
- **Outcome**: Levels, Cloud Leaderboards, and Sentinel AI.

### Lap 4: Social & Meta Layers (Market-Capture)
- **Focus**: Community and Personalization.
- **Outcome**: Ghost Racing, Avatars, and Thematic Skins.

### Lap 5: The Expansion (Platformization)
- **Focus**: UGC and Advanced AI.
- **Outcome**: Level Editor, Behavior-Tree AI, and Daily Challenges.
